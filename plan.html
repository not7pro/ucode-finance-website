SERVICE EXPANSION MATRIX

1. AI WhatsApp & Instagram Lead Bots

Build custom automated conversational agents for local businesses (cafes, clothing stores, bakeries). They answer customer queries, show menus, and take reservations 24/7.

Pricing: 75,000 LKR setup + 25,000 LKR/mo

Instead of charging 100k once for a website, charge 25k/mo on a 12-month contract including hosting, unlimited small content changes, and monthly speed/SEO optimizations.

Combine video editing with TikTok / Meta ad management. You shoot/edit 8 hooks per month, test ad spend, and charge a base retainer plus 10% of ad spend or revenue generated.

Pricing: 120,000 LKR/mo + Performance Bonus
